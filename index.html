Q&A

Questions dentist-owners usually ask first.

Simple answers for the concerns that matter before trusting an assistant with supplier spend.

Will Marginr tell me to buy lower-quality products?

No. The first focus is clean margin leakage: price creep, duplicate charges, overpayments, and changes on products you already buy.

Do I need to switch accounting systems?

No. The V0 starts with supplier invoice uploads. Future app and API integrations are planned as the product matures.

How much time does it take?

The goal is a one-minute review: see the issue, check the evidence, then approve or dismiss the opportunity.

Is it fully launched?

Not yet. Marginr is in development, and early access users help shape the workflow, accuracy, and integrations.
