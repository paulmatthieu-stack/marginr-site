This Privacy Policy explains how Marginr collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you visit https://marginr.com/ or interact with the Marginr early-access website.

Current scope Marginr is currently an early-stage project operated by Paul-Matthieu Castro, based in Lyon, France. Marginr is being built for independent dental practices and is currently in development. This Privacy Policy applies only to the public Marginr website and early-access communications. It does not yet cover the future Marginr application, invoice-upload product, paid service, API, or any customer dashboard. Those services will require a separate privacy policy and, where appropriate, additional agreements.

1. Who controls your information

For the purposes of this website, the data controller is:

Paul-Matthieu Castro, operating Marginr
Lyon, France
Email: paulmatthieu@marginr.com

2. Who this website is for

Marginr is intended for business use by dental practices, dental-practice owners, and related professional users.

The website is not intended for patients, children, or individual consumers seeking dental care. Please do not submit patient information, health information, medical records, treatment details, or protected health information through the website.

3. Information we collect

At this stage, the website collects limited information.

Information you provide directly

If you apply for founding access or contact us, we may collect:

  • Your work email address;
  • Any information you voluntarily provide in a form, email, or direct communication;
  • Your practice or business information, if a future form asks for it and you choose to provide it.

Applying for founding access does not automatically subscribe you to a newsletter.

Information collected automatically

At the time this policy is published, Marginr does not use advertising tracking cookies.

We may later use analytics tools to understand website traffic and improve the website. This may include information such as:

  • Pages visited;
  • Referring websites;
  • Device and browser information;
  • Approximate location based on IP address;
  • Date and time of visits;
  • Website interaction data.

If we enable analytics cookies or similar technologies that require consent, we will update this Privacy Policy and, where required, display a cookie banner or consent mechanism.

4. How we use information

We use website information to:

  • Manage founding-access requests;
  • Respond to inquiries;
  • Communicate with potential early users;
  • Understand interest in Marginr;
  • Improve the website and product direction;
  • Protect the website from misuse, spam, or security issues;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not use website visitor information for third-party AI model training.

5. Founder-access communications

If you provide your work email to request founding access, we may use it to contact you about Marginr, your request, product access, onboarding, feedback, or relevant updates about the early-access process.

Submitting your email for founding access does not mean you have subscribed to a marketing newsletter.

If we later launch a newsletter or promotional email list, we will ask for a separate opt-in where required.

6. Legal bases for processing

Where GDPR or similar privacy laws apply, we process personal information based on one or more of the following legal bases:

  • Pre-contractual steps: to respond to your request for access or information;
  • Legitimate interests: to operate, improve, and protect the website and communicate with professional users who expressed interest;
  • Consent: where required, for optional cookies, analytics, or marketing communications;
  • Legal obligations: where we need to comply with applicable laws.

7. Tools and service providers

We may use trusted service providers to operate the website and manage communications, including:

  • Custom backend infrastructure;
  • Supabase, for backend/database infrastructure;
  • MailerLite, for managing early-access email records or communications;
  • Google Analytics, if enabled in the future for website analytics;
  • Meta Pixel, only if enabled in the future for advertising or retargeting.

These providers may process information on our behalf only for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy or as otherwise required by law.

We do not currently share your information with dental suppliers, distributors, procurement partners, accountants, or other third parties for their own marketing or commercial purposes.

8. Cookies and analytics

At the time this policy is published, Marginr does not use advertising cookies or ad-tracking pixels.

We may later use cookies or similar technologies for analytics, website performance, security, or product improvement.

If we use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or similar tools in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and provide any required consent options before placing non-essential cookies or trackers.

You may also be able to control cookies through your browser settings.

9. International data transfers

Marginr is operated from France and is intended initially for U.S. dental practices.

Some service providers may process or store information outside France, the European Union, or your country of residence. Where required, we will rely on appropriate safeguards, contractual protections, or other lawful transfer mechanisms.

10. Data retention

We keep website and early-access information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

In practice, this means we may keep your founding-access request while Marginr is being developed, while we are communicating with you, or while there is a reasonable business need to maintain the early-access list.

You can request deletion of your information at any time by contacting:

paulmatthieu@marginr.com

If you request deletion, we will delete or anonymize your information unless we need to retain it for legal, security, or legitimate business reasons.

11. Security

Marginr is in an early stage. We aim to protect personal information using reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the limited website data currently collected.

However, no website, email system, database, or online service is completely secure. You should not submit sensitive information, patient information, medical information, or confidential practice records through the public website.

Before launching the Marginr app, invoice-upload workflows, or customer accounts, Marginr should implement and document additional security measures appropriate for business customer data.

12. No patient or health information through the website

The Marginr website is not designed to collect patient information, health information, dental treatment information, or protected health information.

Please do not submit such information through any website form, email, or early-access request.

The future Marginr application may involve supplier invoices and dental-practice operational data. That future product will require separate privacy terms, security terms, and potentially healthcare-specific agreements.

13. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have rights over your personal information, including the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate information;
  • Request deletion of your information;
  • Object to certain processing;
  • Restrict certain processing;
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • Request portability of your information where applicable.

To exercise your rights, contact:

paulmatthieu@marginr.com

We may need to verify your identity before responding.

If you are in the European Union and believe your rights have not been respected, you may also contact your local data protection authority. In France, this is the CNIL.

14. Children

The website is intended for business users and is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

15. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Marginr develops, especially if we add analytics, advertising pixels, new forms, newsletters, user accounts, the Marginr app, invoice uploads, or third-party integrations.

The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
